## Question

`VISION.md` §4 tentatively names "người tự học lập trình qua YouTube free"
as the Stage 2 community. Is this the right first wedge, or is there a
sharper/more reachable one? What's the concrete, specific plan to actually
reach and recruit this community (not just "share-link virality") — e.g.
specific communities/forums/Discords to seed in, a specific content/creator
angle? Decide and record enough specificity that Checkpoint 2 has a real
go-to-community plan, not just a demographic label.

## Resolution

**Decided (founder-confirmed): a VN-first wedge — "người Việt tự học lập
trình qua YouTube" — replaces the broad "self-taught devs" label.**
[Ticket 15](15-wedge-community-research.md)'s research found the broad
label has no home community anywhere, while the VN wedge uniquely has
three properties at once: competitor-free for the specific mechanic
(structure/progress over *arbitrary* free YouTube content —
F8/fullstack.edu.vn validated the model but only for its own catalog),
promo-tolerant channels (FB groups welcome "here's what I made" posts;
~61% of relevant English subreddits structurally don't), and dense
native-language reach (~45k + ~630k in two seeds).

**Concrete go-to-community plan:**

1. Seed FB group "Tự học lập trình miễn phí" (~45k), then J2TEAM
   Community (~630k, follow rules.j2team.org), posting the artifact +
   story per group rules.
2. Follow with a Viblo write-up and a low-key VOZ thread for long-tail
   reach.
3. Global channels (Show HN, r/learnprogramming Saturday thread,
   CS50/Odin Discords) only after the VN retention gate passes.

**Two founder-flagged constraints for the synthesis ticket (10):**

- **The launch artifact is a Checkpoint 1 deliverable, not a GTM
  detail**: 3–5 pre-built public courses from popular free YouTube
  curricula must exist before any outreach — they are what makes the
  first post concrete and shareable instead of an abstract "I built a
  tool" pitch.
- **"Go global only after VN retention signal" is a real gate**:
  Checkpoint 2's success criteria must name a specific VN retention
  number that unlocks global channels — otherwise VN-first drifts into
  "both simultaneously" by default.
